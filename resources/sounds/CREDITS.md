# Sounds shipped with Koh-Vibe

`Attente.wav` and `Fin.wav` are `drop_003.wav` and `drop_004.wav` from
[Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) — public domain, no condition
attached. They are renamed after what they announce: one when a session starts waiting for
you, the other when it finishes.

CC0 asks for nothing in return. The credit is here because the work deserves it, and because
anyone unpacking the extension is owed a straight answer about where its two audio files come
from.

The rest of that library — a hundred sounds — is not shipped: Koh-Vibe offers to download it
once, into `~/.koh-vibe/sounds`. See the *Sound library* row in the settings.
